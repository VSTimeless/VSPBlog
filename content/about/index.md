## Hello, I'm Volodymyr!

I'm an Electrical Engineering student passionate about creating innovative solutions through hardware and software integration. My journey in the world of electronics began when I was a child, taking apart old radios and trying to understand how they worked.

Today, I'm focused on developing my skills in embedded systems, circuit design, and IoT applications. I believe that technology has the power to solve many of the world's challenges, and I'm excited to be part of that solution.

### My Approach

I approach engineering with a balance of creativity and analytical thinking. I enjoy the process of identifying problems, brainstorming solutions, and bringing ideas to life through careful design and implementation. Whether it's designing a PCB for a specific application, writing efficient code for microcontrollers, or connecting devices to form a smart system, I find satisfaction in making things work.
