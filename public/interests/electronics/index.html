Electronics

My Passion for Electronics

Electronics has been my passion since childhood when I first connected a battery to a small light bulb and witnessed the magic of electricity. That simple moment sparked a lifelong fascination with electronic systems.

Areas of Interest

Circuit Design

I enjoy the process of designing circuits, from simple analog configurations to more complex digital systems. Understanding how components interact and creating efficient designs is both challenging and rewarding.

PCB Layout

Turning circuit schematics into physical printed circuit boards is like bringing a design to life. I’ve worked with various PCB design tools including KiCad, Eagle, and Altium Designer, with KiCad being my preferred open-source option.

Embedded Systems

Combining hardware and software in embedded systems allows for creating intelligent devices that interact with the physical world. I work primarily with:

  • Arduino ecosystem for rapid prototyping
  • ESP32/ESP8266 for IoT applications
  • STM32 microcontrollers for more demanding projects
  • Raspberry Pi for projects requiring more processing power

Electronics Test Equipment

I’ve developed a deep appreciation for quality test equipment. My current lab setup includes:

  • Rigol DS1054Z Oscilloscope
  • Aneng AN8008 Multimeter
  • DIY bench power supply
  • Logic analyzer
  • Soldering station

Current Projects

I’m currently working on several electronics projects, including:

  • Building a custom oscilloscope based on an STM32 microcontroller
  • Designing a modular Eurorack synthesizer system
  • Creating various home automation devices

Electronics is more than just a hobby for me—it’s a way to understand and interact with the world. Through electronics, I can turn ideas into physical reality, solve problems, and continuously learn new skills.
